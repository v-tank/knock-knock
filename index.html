Never miss the start of a meeting.

Knock Knock watches your calendar and rings you a minute before each meeting, like an incoming call. Join with one click, or snooze it.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Rings before it starts

A small window opens in the corner of your screen and rings, one minute before (or whenever you choose).

Join or snooze

Join opens the meeting link straight away. Snooze 1, 2 or 5 minutes, and it knocks again.

Private by design

Read-only calendar access. Your events stay in your browser: no servers, no tracking.

How it works

Install the extension and sign in with Google. Knock Knock checks your primary calendar every few minutes and schedules a ring for every video meeting you haven't declined (Google Meet links today). If a meeting moves, so does the ring. Unanswered rings snooze themselves, and they stop 10 minutes after the meeting starts.

Install

Knock Knock works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers, and in Firefox and Zen. Store links are coming soon.
